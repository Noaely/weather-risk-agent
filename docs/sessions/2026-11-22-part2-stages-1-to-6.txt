  How the text check works

    "7.1" and "about 7".

    the years covered, the hub count), so "12 hubs" and "2016-2025" aren't
    flagged. Numbers from your own question aren't flagged either.
  - What it returns: any number that matches nothing goes into
    grounding.text_unverified in the response. It never blocks or retries the
    answer.
  - Tests: 4 new ones:

    - numbers from your question aren't flagged
    - ranges and thousands separators are read correctly
  - Live check: the Miami/Houston and Dallas answers came back with nothing

  - Limitation: numbers in the text are matched against every hub's tool
    results, not the hub the sentence is about. That's documented in
